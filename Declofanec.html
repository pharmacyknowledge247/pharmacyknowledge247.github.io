Diclofenac

Complete Drug Information

Home Medicines Diseases Pharmacology Drug Categories About Contact

Drug Category

NSAID (Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug)

Uses

• Joint Pain
• Muscle Pain
• Arthritis
• Back Pain
• Dental Pain

Mechanism of Action

Diclofenac inhibits COX enzymes and reduces prostaglandin synthesis, helping relieve pain and inflammation.

Adult Dose

Dose depends on the formulation and medical condition. Use only as prescribed by a healthcare professional.

Side Effects

Stomach pain, nausea, acidity, dizziness, stomach ulcer and bleeding (especially with prolonged use).

Precautions

Use with caution in patients with stomach ulcers, kidney disease, liver disease or heart disease. Avoid long-term use unless advised by a doctor.
