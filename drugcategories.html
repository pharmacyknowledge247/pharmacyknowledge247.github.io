Drug Categories

💊 Analgesics

Medicines used to relieve pain.

🦠 Antibiotics

Medicines used to treat bacterial infections.

🤒 Antipyretics

Medicines used to reduce fever.

🌿 Antihistamines

Medicines used to treat allergic conditions.

❤️ Antihypertensives

Medicines used to control high blood pressure.

🩸 Antidiabetics

Medicines used to control blood sugar levels.

🧪 Antifungals

Medicines used to treat fungal infections.

🦠 Antivirals

Medicines used to treat viral infections.

💉 Vaccines

Medicines used to prevent infectious diseases.
