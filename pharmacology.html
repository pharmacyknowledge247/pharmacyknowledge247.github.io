Pharmacology Topics

General Pharmacology

Study of drugs, their actions, uses, side effects and dosage.

Pharmacokinetics (ADME)

Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism and Excretion of drugs.

Pharmacodynamics

Study of how drugs produce their effects in the body.

Routes of Drug Administration

Oral, IV, IM, SC, Topical, Sublingual, Rectal and Inhalation routes.

Adverse Drug Reactions (ADR)

Harmful or unwanted effects produced after taking medicines.

Drug Interactions

When one medicine changes the effect of another medicine.

Half-life (t½)

Time required for the amount of a drug in the body to reduce by 50%.

Bioavailability

The fraction of an administered drug that reaches systemic circulation.

Therapeutic Index

Measurement of a drug's safety by comparing effective and toxic doses.
