General Pharmacology

Study of drugs, their actions, uses, side effects and dosage.

Pharmacokinetics

How the body absorbs, distributes, metabolizes and excretes drugs (ADME).

Pharmacodynamics

How drugs produce their effects in the body.

Routes of Drug Administration

Oral, IV, IM, SC, Topical, Sublingual, Rectal and Inhalation routes.

Adverse Drug Reactions (ADR)

Unwanted or harmful effects that occur after taking a medicine.

Drug Interactions

Effects produced when one medicine changes the action of another medicine.
