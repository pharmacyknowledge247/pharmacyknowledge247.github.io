Aspirin

Complete Drug Information

Home Medicines Diseases Pharmacology Drug Categories About Contact

Drug Category

NSAID, Analgesic, Antipyretic & Antiplatelet

Uses

• Pain Relief
• Fever
• Inflammation
• Prevention of Heart Attack and Stroke (Low Dose)

Mechanism of Action

Aspirin irreversibly inhibits COX-1 and COX-2 enzymes, reducing prostaglandin and thromboxane synthesis.

Side Effects

Stomach irritation, nausea, bleeding risk and allergic reactions.

Precautions

Avoid use in children with viral infections, active stomach ulcers and aspirin allergy. Use only under medical advice.
