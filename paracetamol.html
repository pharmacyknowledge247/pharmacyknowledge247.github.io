Paracetamol

Complete Drug Information

Home Medicines Pharmacology About

Drug Category

Non-Opioid Analgesic & Antipyretic

Uses

• Fever
• Headache
• Toothache
• Body Pain
• Mild to Moderate Pain

Mechanism of Action

Paracetamol inhibits prostaglandin synthesis mainly in the central nervous system, reducing pain and fever.

Dose

Adults: 500–1000 mg every 4–6 hours.
Maximum: 4 g/day.

Side Effects

Nausea, Skin Rash, Liver Damage in Overdose.

Precautions

Avoid overdose. Use carefully in liver disease. Do not combine multiple paracetamol-containing medicines.
