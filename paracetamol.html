💊 Paracetamol

Uses

Paracetamol is used to reduce fever and relieve mild to moderate pain.

Dose

Adults: 500–1000 mg every 4–6 hours as advised by a doctor.

Side Effects

  • Nausea
  • Skin rash
  • Liver damage (in overdose)

Precautions

Do not exceed the recommended dose. Avoid alcohol and consult a doctor if you have liver disease.
